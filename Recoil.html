Win a Six-Piece Rifle Upgrade Package from UNITY Tactical & RECOIL

UNITY Tactical has built its reputation by questioning the assumptions baked into conventional rifle setups. Standard mount height? Reconsider it. Traditional magnifier placement? Build a better system. Familiar controls? Optimize them around the way shooters actually work under pressure.

Now RECOIL and UNITY Tactical are giving one winner the opportunity to overhaul a rifle with six purpose-built components engineered around speed, ergonomics and the human factor.

The complete prize package carries a combined MSRP of $1,120 and includes a FAST FTC 4X magnifier, FAST Optic Riser, AXON SL switch, GASCAP USB-C Tailcap, VFG for M-LOK and SPARK marking light.

The Centerpiece: FAST FTC 4X

At the heart of the package is the black FAST FTC 4X magnifier, valued at $746.

Rather than hanging off the side of the rifle when it isn’t in use, UNITY’s flip-to-center design allows the magnifier to rotate down and out of the sight picture. When magnification is needed, it flips back into position without requiring the optic to be re-zeroed.

It’s a more streamlined approach that keeps the magnifier within the footprint of the weapon platform and reduces the potential for snagging. The integrated assembly is machined from 7075-T6 aluminum and finished with a Type III hardcoat anodizing.

FAST Optic Riser

The FAST Optic Riser, valued at $105, raises a lower one-third co-witness red-dot optic to a 2.26-inch optical centerline, matching the height of the FAST FTC 4X.

That elevated position promotes a more natural heads-up posture and can make it easier to acquire the optic while wearing ear protection, communications gear, gas masks or night-vision equipment. Like the magnifier assembly, the black riser is machined from 7075-T6 aluminum and protected by a Type III hardcoat anodized finish.

AXON SL Remote Switch

The black AXON SL, valued at $114, is an ergonomic single-button remote switch designed to make weapon-light activation fast and repeatable.

Its 45-degree angled control face provides a natural contact point for stress indexing, helping the shooter locate and activate the switch by feel without introducing unnecessary controls or bulk.

GASCAP USB-C Tailcap

The AXON SL is paired with UNITY’s black GASCAP USB-C Tailcap, valued at $109.

GASCAP is designed to provide near-zero-loss output through its USB-C interface while retaining a full-size clicky button for direct light activation. The connection eliminates the need for the bulky plug traditionally found on remote-switch tailcaps, producing a cleaner and more streamlined setup.

VFG for M-LOK

The package also includes the black VFG for M-LOK, valued at $28.

This direct-mount vertical foregrip and hand stop features 360-degree sloped sides, allowing it to support the shooter’s grip from virtually any angle. Its compact form provides a consistent index point without consuming excessive rail space.

SPARK Marking Light

Rounding out the package is a red SPARK marking light, valued at $18.

This compact, fully sealed signal light attaches using hook-and-loop material and delivers more than 65 hours of runtime. It can be used to identify personnel, bags, equipment or other mission-critical items in low-light environments.

Six Components. One Complete Upgrade.

The winner will receive:

  • FAST FTC 4X magnifier in black — MSRP $746
  • FAST Optic Riser in black — MSRP $105
  • AXON SL switch in black — MSRP $114
  • GASCAP USB-C Tailcap in black — MSRP $109
  • VFG for M-LOK in black — MSRP $28
  • SPARK marking light in red — MSRP $18

Total prize value: $1,120

How to Enter

Submit your email address and agree to receive email newsletters from the contest sponsors to receive your first entry.

Once registered, you’ll have opportunities to earn additional entries by visiting or following the sponsors on social media and referring friends to the giveaway.

The giveaway opens Friday, August 28, 2026, and closes Wednesday, September 30, 2026. One winner will receive the complete six-piece UNITY Tactical rifle upgrade package.

If your current build could benefit from a faster optic configuration, better light controls and more deliberate ergonomics, get your entry in before the deadline.
