TRAVELSAVERS Canada helping members deliver ideas, practical advice and compelling offers to their clients

By Bob Mowat

September 8, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article

When you ask Jane Clementino, Senior Vice President and General Manager of TRAVELSAVERS Canada how the group’s members are managing their way through the ‘interesting times’ we’re all living in, she’ll tell you: “Our advisors are navigating a lot right now, from airline disruptions and geopolitical uncertainty to rising costs and clients who are being more cautious about where and when they travel.”

But she’ll also point out that: “At the same time, clients are arriving with more ideas and information from AI and social media. Our advisors are doing an excellent job of taking that initial inspiration, understanding what the client truly wants and turning it into a realistic, personalized and well-planned trip.”

It’s a wide-ranging conversation that covers a lot of ground, and you can check out the full story in the Aug. 31, 2026 issue of CANADIAN TRAVEL PRESS.
