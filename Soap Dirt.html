Big Brother Spoilers: Dee SPINS & HUSTLES Votes – Yash Questions the Pact

September 8, 2026 Belynda Gates-Turner 11 Views Angela Murray, Barrett Pfeiffer, BB28, Dee Valladares, Drew Campbell, Melody Morris, Rick Devens, Taylor Brown, Yash Patel

It’s time for a Big Brother spoilers update including info on the Labor Day party, how hard Dee Valladares‘s campaigning, Yash Patel reconsidering his endgame allies, and some sizzle between Drew Campbell and Melody Morris after they were served alcohol by production.

So first, it’s confirmed BB Blockbuster ends at final 6, so we have got two more weeks of that comp before real blindsides begin. For Labor Day, the houseguests got fun stuff. A Jenga set, booze, and a lot of food. Barbecue stuff.

And the Have Nots got to eat which was a treat. The Jenga’s been a big focus of today’s feeds. That and Drew getting in his father-son time with Rick Devens.

Big Brother: Angela Targets Barrett

Dee and Angela Murray talked about Barrett. Dee said she’d make sure Barrett Pfeiffer regrets this for the rest of his life. Angela called Barrett’s move a $750,000 mistake.

Taylor Brown wants Yash to win HOH next week and nominate Drew, Melody and Barrett. That leaves Taylor and two icons off the block. If there’s a renom and Yash is HOH, Taylor would be the renom unless she won veto and saved Melody.

Then Yash could renom an icon. Barrett’s hoping that Melody or Drew win the next HOH so he’ll be safe since Barrett can’t play that comp. And once the houseguests see this week isn’t the double eviction, they should figure out it’s next week.

And Yash told Taylor in the double eviction, he wants to put up Drew and Melody and evict Drew. Speaking of Drew, he tattled to Devens and Dee that the others were planning to do a mocking hand puppet thing at the veto meeting.

The other Ceasefire 4 will be ticked if they find out. Barrett may have overheard them. Drew also told Devens he made a mistake and should’ve nominated Melody on his HOH. Drew also told Devens about the Ceasefire alliance.

Drew said the newbies called a ceasefire until the icons are all out. Drew also told Devens that he’s the only one in the house he trusts. He asks what about Melody and Drew said he doesn’t trust her like he trusts Devens.

Drew also told Devens he won’t vote to evict him or Dee, just Angela. Of course, if it’s Devens and Dee on the block Thursday night because Angela wins Blockbuster, Drew will have to vote one of them out.

Big Brother 28: Drew’s Got Moves

Drew is campaigning to Devens as hard as if Drew were on the block this week. It may be a good game move. Drew also told Devens that if he wins HOH, he’ll nominate Yash and Taylor. Drew is never ever going to give up on daddy Devens.

Plus, Drew really wants to see Yash evicted. Those two are gunning hard for each other. Taylor and Yash made a final two deal. Also, Dee told Devens she won’t campaign against either him or Angela.

That came up when Devens tried to convince Dee to work with him and campaign against Angela. Dee said she wasn’t going to do it and doesn’t want to hear about it. Devens said it won’t be anything negative but Dee said don’t even tell me.

She told Devens “it’s not my problem”. Yet Dee later cam-talked and plotted how to convince Yash to keep her over Angela. Dee plans to say Angela can’t win comps but Dee can, so she can help him.

She also plans to tell Yash if they’re together on the block, Dee’s a bigger target for eviction. Dee said she’ll tell Yash she’s done with the alliance and will just be loyal to Yash. And she wants to tell Yash if he keeps Angela, he’ll be outnumbered.

Dee cam talked about making a logical argument. After working out her approach in her head, she went to Yash. And Dee told Yash that Melody, Drew and Barrett want to keep Angela.

And Dee said that Yash is playing their game if he goes along with it. Dee also planned to talk to Taylor and may be looking at a three-way alliance with her, Yash and Taylor.

Big Brother: Dee Lies to Devens

So, Dee, I suspect, might’ve lied to Devens’ face because despite what she told him, Dee’s campaigning hard. Dee also told Yash if he and Taylor vote to keep her, the other icon will also vote to keep her, whether it’s Angela or Devens.

Dee also said she’s done with Drew and Barrett and told Yash point blank that both those guys want him out. By the way, Devens talked to Yash after Dee and made a nearly identical pitch but didn’t present it nearly as well as Dee.

Speaking of votes, Barrett cam talked and said he thinks he can beat anyone at final two. And Angela came to campaign to him. Barrett told her Drew wasn’t the one behind him nominating the icons. Angela threw Drew under the bus anyway.

Also fun on the feeds, Taylor was doing cartwheels and flips. Everyone applauded but Big Brother said “stop that” and Taylor sassed back that you can’t do anything around here.

Drew and Melody still haven’t kissed on camera but on the feeds, he walked by her in the kitchen and Drew put his hand on Melody’s bottom. And they were in the pool yesterday alone although others were nearby.

And some fans think there were things going on below the water. I watched the clip and couldn’t tell either way. But Angela was sitting on the side of the pool, behind them and could see what was going on.

Labor Day Libations on BB28

Drew and Melody were tipsy because Big Brother served booze for Labor Day. Angela said Melody was rubbing her foot up Drew’s leg in the pool. Angela said he was doing the same to Melody.

And she said  Drew was rubbing on her waist under water. Yet, Melody seems to hate Drew, then forgives him. So, it’s love to hate him thing, it seems. Also yesterday, since Melody’s a Have Not on cold showers, Drew got in the shower on the other side of the curtain and cranked it up hot hoping to warm her up.

After Melody got out, she teased Drew and said she was going to open the curtain. He said are you for real and Melody said no. Drew said that would’ve been bad while the look on his face was like, whoa…
